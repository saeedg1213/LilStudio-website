How I Build

The same four rules apply to everything here.

One job, done properly

Every app is scoped to a single annoyance. No dashboards, no feature creep, no onboarding wizard.

Your data stays put

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. Nothing leaves your machine unless the app literally can't work without it.

Pay once, or nothing

Free where it can be free, one small payment where it can't. No subscriptions for a utility that sits in your menu bar.

Native, not a web page

Swift and SwiftUI, built for the platform. Small binaries, low CPU, no Electron shell eating your battery.

Got an idea, or a bug?

Feature requests, bug reports, or just to say the thing worked — mail lands with me directly.
