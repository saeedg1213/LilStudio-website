Dim or Blur

Light & Dark Profiles

One Window, or the Whole App

Highlight just the front window, or every window of the front app. Choose per app, too.

Made for Big Setups

Nothing Wrongly Dimmed

Save sheets, dialogs, menus, popovers and tooltips stay bright, with perfect corners and shadows. Launchers and menu bar popovers never flash the screen.

Focus Ring

Add a colored border around the window you're in. Use it with the dim, or on its own at 0% intensity.

URL scheme

From Terminal, Raycast, Alfred or a Stream Deck button.

open "lildim://toggle"
open "lildim://intensity?value=40"
open "lildim://pause?minutes=30"

AppleScript

Read and set LilDim from any script.

tell application "LilDim"
    set intensity to 50
    pause dimming for 15
end tell

Good to Know

Does LilDim need any permissions?

No. It works the moment you open it, using only the public window list (positions and sizes, never titles or contents). Accessibility is optional: with it, LilDim follows focus changes instantly and knows exactly which of an app's windows is focused, and fn-to-peek turns on.

I can't find LilDim in the Accessibility list.

macOS doesn't let App Store apps add themselves there. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, click + and choose LilDim (or drag it in from Finder), then switch it on. LilDim's Settings walks you through it and turns green the moment it's done.

Will it dim my menus, dialogs or Save sheets?

No. The dim sits directly behind your window in the system's window order, so everything that belongs to it (sheets, menus, popovers, tooltips) stays above it and bright. Moving or resizing the window costs no CPU at all.

Is it heavy on battery?

No. In our tests it sat at about 0.1% CPU while idle without Accessibility, around 25 MB of memory, and the app is about 2 MB. Blur switches to dim on its own in Low Power Mode.

Coming from HazeOver?

Everything you used is here: intensity, color, fade speed, Light and Dark profiles, front window or all app windows, multi-display modes, Focus Filters, Shortcuts, AppleScript and hold fn. LilDim adds blur, a focus ring, per-app pause rules and a URL scheme, for $0.99.

I hid the menu bar icon. How do I get back?

Open LilDim again from Finder, Spotlight or Launchpad and Settings appears.

Why do screenshots and recordings show the dim?

Because it's really on screen. Pause LilDim (or hold fn) while you capture if you want the full desktop.
