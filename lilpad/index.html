    Features

    Nothing on the Mac

    LilPad uses the Screen Sharing that's already built into macOS. No helper app, no driver, no menu bar icon of ours.

    Built for low lag

    Raw touches, no gesture-recognizer delay, a few bytes per movement, and a radio that stays awake while you work.

    Finds your Mac

    Macs with Screen Sharing on show up by themselves. Sign in once and LilPad reconnects every time you open it.

    Lock it in place

    One tap hides the controls and pins the orientation. Hold to unlock, so a stray swipe never opens a menu.

    The whole screen

    Edge to edge, around the notch, in portrait or either landscape. Every point of glass is trackpad.

    Day & Night

    A cream day theme and a warm dark night theme, with haptics on every click and gesture.

    Set Up in a Minute

    Once. After that, just open the app.

    1

    Turn on Screen Sharing

    On your Mac: System Settings → General → Sharing, then switch on Screen Sharing.

    2

    Same network

    Put your iPhone and Mac on the same Wi‑Fi. No router around? Join your iPhone's Personal Hotspot from the Mac.

    3

    Pick your Mac

    Open LilPad, tap your Mac, and sign in with your Mac username and password. It's saved in your iPhone's Keychain.

    Good to Know

    Why Screen Sharing?

    It's the one door into a Mac that needs no extra software. LilPad only sends pointer and keyboard-shortcut events through it. It never asks for your screen image, so the traffic is tiny.

    Why not Bluetooth?

    iOS doesn't let apps act as a Bluetooth mouse or trackpad. Every trackpad app on the App Store works over the network for the same reason.

    Is my password safe?

    It's stored in your iPhone's Keychain and sent only to your Mac, encrypted with Apple's own Screen Sharing sign-in. It never goes anywhere else.

    Can I use it with a mouse?

    Yes, that's the idea. One thing to know: after you move the mouse, the pointer jumps back to where LilPad left it the next time you touch the iPhone.

    Is it exactly a Magic Trackpad?

    Close. Pointer, clicks and drags feel native. Scrolling moves in small wheel steps, and Mission Control, Spaces and zoom are sent as their standard Mac shortcuts.

    What's that icon in my menu bar?

    macOS shows a Screen Sharing icon while LilPad is connected. That's expected, and it goes away when you close the app.
