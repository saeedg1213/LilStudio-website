    Features

    One small menu, everything you'd want from a keyboard you don't have.

    Recorded, Then Cleaned

    Real takes with the room noise lifted out by spectral gating, trimmed to the attack, levelled so releases sit under presses like on a real board.

    Smart Dynamics

    Soft, medium and hard layers chosen from your typing rhythm, with Return and Space a little heavier and fast typists a little lighter.

    Stereo & 3D Audio

    Keys on the left sound on the left. In headphones, 3D audio puts the keyboard in front of you instead of inside your head.

    Quiet in Calls

    When any app uses your microphone, LilClack pauses itself, and it comes back when you hang up. Focus filters can switch it off too.

    On and Off, Your Way

    A shortcut you choose (⌃⌥K out of the box), a right-click on the icon, the Shortcuts app, or a lilclack://toggle link.

    Tiny and Instant

    About 2 MB, native Swift, sounds pre-loaded so a key plays right away. Turned off, it stops listening and uses nothing at all.

    Good to Know

    Does it record what I type?

    No. LilClack only learns that a key went down or up and where that key sits (for stereo). It never reads characters, keeps no log, and has no internet access at all.

    Why does it ask for Input Monitoring?

    That's the macOS permission for noticing key presses in other apps. LilClack uses a listen-only tap: it can't change, block or send keystrokes. The welcome window walks you through it.

    Will people on my calls hear it?

    Not by default. "Pause during calls" silences LilClack whenever an app is using your microphone. It checks only whether the mic is in use; it never opens it.

    Does it work with external keyboards?

    Yes, with the built-in keyboard and any keyboard you plug in or pair. It can play through any output, including AirPods, and follows you when you switch.

    How much does it cost?

    $1, once. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no account. It's on its way to the Mac App Store.
