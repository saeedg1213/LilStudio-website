Privacy Policy

LilClack for macOS — last updated 21 November 2026

LilClack collects nothing. No personal data, no usage data, no analytics, no crash reports, no identifiers. The app has no server, no account system, and no network connection.

What LilClack notices

To play a sound for each key press, LilClack uses a listen-only keyboard event tap, which macOS allows after you grant Input Monitoring. For each event it reads only that a key went down or up, the key's position on the keyboard (to place the sound left or right), and whether it was a modifier such as Shift. It never reads the characters you type, never keeps a log of keys, and cannot change, block or send keystrokes. If you turn on mouse sounds, it notices that a mouse button was pressed, nothing more.

Pause during calls

With "Pause during calls" on, LilClack asks macOS whether your default microphone is currently being used by any app. That's a yes-or-no property of the device. LilClack never opens the microphone and never hears or records audio.

What LilClack stores on your Mac

LilClack saves your settings (sound, volume, tone, options, shortcut) and two simple counters, keys pressed today and in total, using the standard macOS preferences system. The counters are numbers only, with no record of which keys. Nothing leaves your computer. Deleting the app removes them.

What LilClack does not do

  • It does not record, store or transmit what you type.
  • It does not open your microphone or camera.
  • It does not request Accessibility, Screen Recording or Full Disk Access.
  • It does not connect to the internet. It's sandboxed, with no network access.
  • It does not include third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising.

Children

LilClack is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes on the Mac App Store.
