LilClack Support

Real mechanical keyboard sounds for your Mac.

Need a hand, found a bug, or want a board added? Email support@lilstudio.app. We read every message and usually reply the same day.

Getting started

LilClack has no Dock icon. After launching, a welcome window helps you allow Input Monitoring, then it lives in your menu bar as a small keyboard.

  • Click the keyboard to pick a board (Clack, Crisp or Snappy), set volume and tone, and open Options.
  • Right-click it, or press ⌃⌥K, to turn sounds on or off.
  • Click the shortcut shown in the menu to record a new one. Esc cancels, Delete removes it.

Common questions

I don't hear anything.

Check that LilClack is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring (the menu shows an orange banner if it isn't). Then check the master switch, the volume slider, and Options › Output. The menu's status line says "Paused, mic in use" while an app is using your microphone and "Pause during calls" is on.

Why does the first key or the spacebar sound different?

On purpose. LilClack reads your typing rhythm: quick rolls play soft takes, ordinary typing medium ones, and the first key after a pause, Return and Space hit harder. Turn off Options › Natural variation for a steadier sound.

Can I use it with AirPods or another speaker?

Yes. "System Output" follows whatever macOS is using, including when AirPods connect. Pick a specific device in Options › Output to keep LilClack there. For headphones, turn on 3D audio.

Can I control it from Shortcuts or a script?

The Shortcuts app has Toggle LilClack, Turn LilClack On or Off, and Set LilClack Switch actions, and a Focus filter can turn sounds off during a Focus. Links work too: lilclack://on, lilclack://off, lilclack://toggle and lilclack://switch/crisp.

How do I quit it?

Click the keyboard in the menu bar and choose Quit (⌘Q while the menu is open). To stop it starting with your Mac, turn off Options › Open at login.

System requirements

macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
