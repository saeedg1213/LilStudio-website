Good to Know

Will the store's scanner read my phone?

Almost every modern checkout scanner reads phone screens, and LilPass turns the brightness up and draws the code crisp. If an old laser scanner won't, the cashier can type the number printed under the barcode.

How is this different from Apple Wallet?

Wallet only holds passes a store has made for it. LilPass holds any card with a barcode or QR code, including the plastic ones that never came with an app.

Does it need internet?

No. Your cards live on your iPhone and every barcode is drawn on the device, so it works in a basement checkout with no signal.

Can I add my student or staff ID?

If it has a barcode or QR code, yes. Library, gym, cafeteria and campus cards often do. Tap-only (NFC) cards can't be copied by any app.

Is it free?

Free for up to 5 cards. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited cards, and it carries over to your other devices with the same Apple Account.

What if I lose my phone?

With iCloud sync on, your cards come back when you sign in on a new iPhone. With it off, they exist only on that device, which is the most private option.
