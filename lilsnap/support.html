Getting started

The first time you open LilSnap, a short guide connects it to Apple's Shortcuts app. It takes two clicks:

  1. Add Shortcut… opens Shortcuts with the LilSnap shortcut. Click Add Shortcut and keep its name, LilSnap.
  2. Allow & Test: when macOS asks whether LilSnap may use Shortcuts Events, click OK.

That's all. LilSnap then lives in your menu bar. Click the icon for a grid of layouts with each key printed on its tile, or choose Keyboard Shortcuts… for the cheat sheet.

The shortcuts

Every default starts with ⌃⌥ (Control-Option). You can change any of them, and give any layout a second shortcut, in Settings → Keyboard.

  • ← → ↑ ↓ halves. Press a half again for ⅔, then ⅓.
  • Two arrows together for a quarter: → + ↑ is the top right.
  • 1 2 3 thirds (also D F G), 4 5 left or right two-thirds (also E T).
  • ↩ maximize (also 0), C center, ⌫ put the window back.
  • ⌘→ / ⌘← next or previous display.
  • ⌘2 ⌘3 ⌘4 tile the front 2, 3 or 4 windows.

Common questions

Windows stopped moving.

LilSnap opens its setup page on its own when this happens. Two things to check: a shortcut named LilSnap is in the Shortcuts app (if you renamed or deleted it, click Add Shortcut… again), and LilSnap is switched on under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation → LilSnap → Shortcuts Events.

The first snap is slow.

After a long break, macOS needs a few seconds to start Shortcuts in the background. After that a snap takes about a fifth of a second.

I see two previews when I drag a window.

That's macOS's own tiling. Turn off Drag windows to screen edges to tile in System Settings → Desktop & Dock. Settings → General has a button that opens it.

A shortcut doesn't work.

Another app has probably claimed it. Settings → Keyboard marks it with a warning; record a different one there.

An app's window doesn't fill the whole area.

Some apps have a minimum size or resize in steps (Terminal, for example). LilSnap keeps those windows glued to the right edges, so they line up as closely as the app allows.

I want LilSnap to leave one app alone.

Open the menu, choose Extra → Ignore with that app in front, or add it in Settings → Apps.

I hid the menu bar icon. How do I get back to Settings?

Open LilSnap again from Finder, Spotlight or Launchpad and Settings appears.

Automation

Shortcuts app: Move Front Window, Tile Front Windows, Restore Front Window, Apply Custom Layout.

URL scheme (from Terminal, launchers, Stream Deck…):

  • lilsnap://action/leftHalf (any layout: rightHalf, topLeft, firstThird, maximize…)
  • lilsnap://tile/3 (2–4)
  • lilsnap://restore
  • lilsnap://custom/Coding (a custom layout by name)
  • lilsnap://settings

How do I stop LilSnap from starting automatically?

Turn off Launch at login in Settings → General. It's off until you turn it on.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit LilSnap from its menu, then drag the app from your Applications folder to the Trash. You can also delete the LilSnap shortcut in the Shortcuts app.
