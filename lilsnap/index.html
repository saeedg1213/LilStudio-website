Features

Everything you reach for every day, and nothing you have to think about.

Snap From the Keyboard

Halves, quarters, thirds, two-thirds, fourths, sixths, center and maximize. Press a half again and it steps to ⅔, then ⅓.

Two Arrows, One Quarter

Hold ⌃⌥ and press → and ↑ together for the top-right quarter. No letters to memorise.

Drag to Snap

Drag a window to an edge or corner and a preview appears right behind it. Drop it at the top center for the Snap Bar with six ready layouts.

Tile 2, 3 or 4 Windows

One shortcut arranges the windows in front. Three windows get one big and two stacked, so no corner of your screen goes to waste.

Your Own Layouts

Draw a layout on a 12×12 grid, give it a shortcut, and it shows up in the menu next to the built-in ones.

Gaps Your Way

Add space between windows and around the edges. Thirds stay exactly equal, gaps or not.

Every Display

Send a window to the next display in one press. Portrait monitors stack layouts top to bottom. Restore puts any window back where it was.

A Menu Bar Icon That Knows

The icon draws the front window's layout. Open it for a tile grid with every shortcut printed right on its tile.

Automation

Actions for the Shortcuts app, plus a lilsnap:// URL scheme for Raycast, Alfred or a Stream Deck.

Light and Private

About 16 MB of memory and 0% CPU while idle. No network access, no account, no tracking. In English and French.

Good to Know

Does LilSnap need Accessibility access?

No. It reads only window positions and sizes, never titles or contents, and moves windows through its shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app. The only question macOS asks is whether LilSnap may use Shortcuts Events.

How fast is a snap?

About a fifth of a second. The first snap after a long break can take a few seconds while macOS starts Shortcuts in the background. Press keys as fast as you like: LilSnap queues them up.

Why does a Shortcuts icon appear in the menu bar when I snap?

That's macOS showing that LilSnap's shortcut just ran. It shows a check mark and goes away by itself. It's how every Mac App Store app that works with Shortcuts behaves.

Coming from Rectangle or Magnet?

Your hands already know it: ⌃⌥ plus the arrows, ↩ to maximize, C to center. Thirds are on 1 2 3 and still on D F G. Every shortcut can be changed.

I see two previews when I drag a window.

macOS has its own drag tiling. Turn off Drag windows to screen edges to tile in System Settings → Desktop & Dock. LilSnap's Settings has a button that takes you there.

Windows stopped moving.

LilSnap opens its setup page by itself when this happens. Check that a shortcut named LilSnap is in the Shortcuts app, and that LilSnap is on under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

I hid the menu bar icon. How do I get back?

Open LilSnap again from Finder, Spotlight or Launchpad and Settings appears.

Is it heavy on battery?

No. In our tests it used about 16 MB of memory, 0% CPU and no wakeups while idle. It does nothing until you press a key or drag a window.
