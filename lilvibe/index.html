Features

A moving desktop

The scene plays behind your icons and windows, on every display and in every Space, and loops without a visible seam.

Sound that doesn't repeat

Two steady layers underneath, and small moments like a bird or a cup placed at random. It never plays back the same way twice.

Easy on your battery

Video is decoded by your Mac's media hardware, once for all screens. It pauses when a full-screen app or a window covers it, and when your Mac sleeps or locks.

Matching wallpaper

Optionally sets a still of the scene as your real wallpaper, so the menu bar, Mission Control and Lock Screen match. Yours comes back when you turn it off.

Just the sound, if you like

Switch the video off and keep a still picture. The ambience carries on and the GPU gets a rest.

Lives in the menu bar

Scenes, play and pause, volume and launch at login, all in one small menu. No window to manage.

Good to Know

Will it drain my battery?

It's built not to. Video runs on the Mac's dedicated media hardware and stops whenever you can't see it: behind a full-screen app, under a maximized window, on a sleeping display or a locked screen. You can also pause it on battery, or switch video off and keep only the sound.

Does it work with more than one display?

Yes. Every connected display shows the scene, and it adjusts when you plug a screen in, unplug it, or change its resolution. The video is decoded once and shared, so a second screen costs very little.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All four scenes and their sounds are inside the app. Nothing is streamed or downloaded after you install it.

What happens to my own wallpaper?

If you turn on wallpaper matching, LilVibe remembers your wallpaper for each display and puts it back when you turn matching off. Your own photos come back perfectly. Some of Apple's built-in wallpapers don't restore correctly because of a macOS bug; if that happens, pick yours again in System Settings → Wallpaper.

Can I mix the sounds myself?

Not yet. Each scene comes with a mix tuned so nothing sticks out. Per-layer sliders are on the list for a later update.
