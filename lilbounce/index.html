Features

Real physics

Gravity follows how you hold the phone. The balls roll, spin, skid and bounce like real ones, 120 times a second.

Tap, hold, throw

Tap a ball to kick it, swipe into one to send it flying, or hold it and drag it around, then let go to throw it. Use every finger.

Seven balls

Rubber, tennis, beach, ping-pong, basketball, superball and bowling, each with its own bounce, sound and weight.

They have faces

They blink, watch your finger, squint on hard hits, get dizzy from spinning, and nod off when you leave them alone.

The whole screen

The balls bounce off the Dynamic Island and roll around it, edge to edge. Pick a room too: slippery ice, soft felt or a springy bouncy one.

Feel every bounce

Strong, proportional haptics and soft sounds made on the fly. A light tap for a nudge, a thump for a drop.

In Your Hand

One ball or thirty, big or small, day or night.

A ball pit

Thirty balls mid-shake, straight off a real iPhone.

Basketballs on felt

Every ball and room plays differently.

Night mode

Dark fields, or follow your phone's setting.

Good to Know

Is it a game?

It's a toy. No levels, no scores, no timers. Just balls to roll around and fidget with whenever you like.

Does it need the internet?

No. LilBounce runs entirely on your device and never connects to anything. It works the same in airplane mode.

Will it drain my battery?

It runs at 120 frames a second while things move, and drops to 30 when everything is still. Close it like any app when you're done.

The taps feel too strong.

Turn them down in settings (the coral button) under Feel → Haptic strength, or switch haptics or sound off.

Can I share a clip?

Yes. Settings → Replay makes a short video of the last 10 seconds, with sound, to save or send.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, in every orientation and in Split View. The bigger the screen, the bigger the playground.
