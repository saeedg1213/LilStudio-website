Privacy Policy

LilBounce for iPhone and iPad — last updated 22 October 2026

LilBounce collects nothing. No personal data, no usage data, no analytics, no crash reports, no identifiers. There is no account, no LilStudio server, and no third-party SDK. The app never connects to the internet.

Motion and touch

LilBounce reads your device's motion sensors (tilt, shake and rotation) and your touches to move the balls. These readings are used live, in memory, and thrown away a moment later. They are never stored and never leave your device. iOS doesn't ask for a permission for this, because no motion history or activity data is involved.

Replay videos

When you tap Share the last 10 seconds, LilBounce makes a short video of the balls on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The video goes only where you choose to send it. If you pick Save Video, iOS asks once for permission to add to your photo library. LilBounce can't see or read your photos.

What LilBounce stores on your device

Your settings (which ball, size and how many, room, night mode, haptics, sound and the physics sliders), the pegs you plant, and whether you've seen the first-run hint are saved locally using the standard iOS preferences system. They never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

What LilBounce does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or to any LilStudio server.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos (it can only add a video you choose to save).
  • It has no accounts, sign-ins or in-app purchases.

Children

LilBounce is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes on the App Store.
