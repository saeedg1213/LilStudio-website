LilDisplay vs OpenDisplay

An honest comparison, as of November 2026

Both apps turn an iPhone or iPad into a real extended display for your Mac, over USB or WiFi, with the same idea: a free Mac app plus an app on the device. They differ in what you can do on that display, and in price.

Pick OpenDisplay if…

  • you only need an extra screen to look at,
  • you want open-source software, or
  • you want to use an old Mac as a display.

Pick LilDisplay if…

  • you want to control your Mac from the screen: Trackpad mode with Spaces and Mission Control gestures, or touch exactly where you want to click,
  • you want to type on the Mac from your phone or tablet, or
  • you connect over WiFi away from a router, on a train, in a café, or in a hotel where devices can't see each other.

The best test is your own: LilDisplay gives you three full sessions free, no account needed.
