LilDisplay vs Sidecar

When the built-in option isn't enough

Sidecar is Apple's own way to use an iPad as a second display. It is free, built into macOS, and very good at what it does. If it works for you, use it.

It has a few limits, though, and that is where LilDisplay comes in.

The cases LilDisplay solves

  • You don't have an iPad, but you have an iPhone.
  • Work Mac, personal iPad: Sidecar needs the same Apple ID on both, and doesn't work with managed Apple IDs.
  • Your iPad or Mac is too old for Sidecar.
  • You want to use your fingers: tap to click, Trackpad mode with Mission Control and Spaces gestures, and typing on the Mac.
