LilDisplay Support

Your iPhone or iPad as a second display for your Mac, with touch and a keyboard.

Stuck, found a bug, or want a feature? Email support@lildisplay.app. We read every message. If something went wrong, tell us your Mac and iPhone or iPad models, their system versions, and whether you use USB or WiFi.

Getting started

  • Install LilDisplay from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad, and the free LilDisplay Mac app from our website.
  • The Mac app has no window. It lives in the menu bar, where it shows the WiFi pairing code.
  • On the iPhone or iPad, swipe up to open the menu, choose USB or WiFi, and tap Connect.

Common problems

It says connected, but the screen stays blank.

The Mac app needs Screen Recording permission to show its display on your device. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and turn on LilDisplay Mac, then quit and reopen it from the menu bar.

Already on, especially right after an update? Choose Fix Permissions After Update… from the LilDisplay menu (it asks for your administrator password), reopen the app, and allow access again when asked.

I can see my Mac, but touches and typing do nothing.

Touch, trackpad and keyboard input need Accessibility permission: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on LilDisplay Mac. It takes effect right away, no restart needed. The Permissions items in the LilDisplay menu show what is missing and open the right page.

WiFi won't connect.

  • The two-digit code on the iPhone or iPad must match the code in the Mac menu bar. The Mac picks a new code every few hours, so check it again if it worked before.
  • Turn Wi-Fi on on both devices. They don't need to join the same network: they connect directly, peer-to-peer.
  • On the iPhone or iPad, allow LilDisplay to use the local network: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → LilDisplay.
  • Still nothing? Choose Reconnect (⌘R) in the Mac menu, or quit and reopen the Mac app.

USB won't connect.

  • Unlock the iPhone or iPad and tap Trust if it asks whether to trust this computer.
  • Use a cable that carries data (some charging-only cables don't), plugged directly into the Mac.
  • Keep LilDisplay open in front on the iPhone or iPad, then choose Reconnect (⌘R) on the Mac.

Swiping between Spaces or opening Mission Control doesn't work.

The 3- and 4-finger swipes use the standard Mission Control keyboard shortcuts. Make sure they are turned on in System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Mission Control (Move left/right a space, Mission Control, Application windows).

On iPad, 4-finger swipes may be taken by iPadOS's own multitasking gestures. Use 3 fingers, or turn those gestures off in the iPad's Settings.

The picture is soft or the pointer lags.

Pick a different setting under Display Quality in the Mac menu: Smooth for the fastest response, Balanced (recommended), Sharp, or Full Resolution for a 1:1 pixel display. USB gives the sharpest picture with the lowest latency; over WiFi, Balanced or Smooth work best.

The app says "Update LilDisplay for Mac".

Your Mac app is older than the iPhone or iPad app expects. Download the latest version from our website, quit LilDisplay from the menu bar, drag the new copy onto Applications and choose Replace. Your permissions are kept.

Rotating my iPhone restarts the connection.

LilDisplay for Mac 3.2 and later resize the display in place when you rotate or fold. Older Mac versions restart and reconnect by themselves, which takes a few seconds. Update the Mac app, or lock the orientation to Portrait or Landscape in the menu on your device.

Notifications stop while I'm connected.

macOS hides notifications while a display is shared. To keep them, go to System Settings → Notifications and turn on Allow notifications when mirroring or sharing the display.

Can I use my MacBook with the lid closed?

Yes. Turn on Advanced → Keep Awake When Lid Is Closed in the LilDisplay menu. Turn it off again before you quit or uninstall the app, so your Mac goes back to sleeping normally when you close the lid.

I bought LilDisplay on another device, or before it was paid.

One purchase covers your iPhone and iPad with the same Apple Account: tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen. If you downloaded LilDisplay before it became a paid app, full access is unlocked for you automatically.

How do I uninstall it?

On the Mac, turn off Keep Awake When Lid Is Closed if you use it, quit LilDisplay from its menu, and drag LilDisplay Mac from Applications to the Trash. On the iPhone or iPad, delete the app as usual.

System requirements

Mac with macOS 14 or later. iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15.6 or later.

Older versions of the Mac app

The download on our home page is always the newest version, and it is the one we recommend. If a new version gives you trouble, you can go back to an earlier one below. It works with the current iPhone and iPad app. Please also tell us what went wrong so we can fix it.

  • LilDisplay Mac 3.2 (October 2026): resizes the display in place when you rotate, Display Quality menu, iPhone Duo support.
  • LilDisplay Mac 3.1 (October 2026): trackpad gestures, typing from the iPhone, steadier WiFi.

To switch versions, quit LilDisplay from its menu, then drag the app from the downloaded DMG into Applications and replace the old one. Your permissions stay as they are.
