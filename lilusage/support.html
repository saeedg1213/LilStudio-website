LilUsage Support

Your Claude usage, in the menu bar.

Stuck, or the meter looks wrong? Email support@lilstudio.app. We read every message.

Getting started

  • Open the DMG and drag LilUsage to Applications, then open it. It has no Dock icon.
  • Click the meter in the menu bar and read the short intro, then Get started.
  • macOS asks to let LilUsage use "Claude Code-credentials". Click Always Allow and enter your Mac password.

Common questions

macOS keeps asking for my password.

You clicked Allow instead of Always Allow. Wait for the next prompt and choose Always Allow. LilUsage 1.1 reads the credentials in a way that keeps that choice, even when Claude Code refreshes its login.

The menu bar says "Sign in".

Your Claude Code login expired or you logged out. Run claude in Terminal, type /login and finish in your browser. The meter comes back within seconds.

The meter shows only a dot.

LilUsage doesn't have numbers yet: it's still loading, or it can't read the credentials. Click it to see why.

Can I see the number instead of just the bar?

Click the meter, then ••• › Show percentage in menu bar.

"Rate limited — retrying in a few minutes".

The usage service asked LilUsage to slow down. It backs off by itself and recovers without you doing anything.

Does it see my conversations?

No. It asks Anthropic for your usage percentages and nothing else. See the privacy policy.

How do I uninstall it?

Click the meter, choose Quit LilUsage, then drag the app from Applications to the Trash.

System requirements

macOS 14.6 or later, Apple silicon or Intel, with Claude Code installed and signed in.
