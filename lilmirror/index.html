How It Works

Three steps, about fifteen seconds.

1

Install and Allow the Camera

Get it from the Mac App Store and open it. The icon appears in your menu bar — there's no window and no Dock icon by design. macOS asks for camera access the first time you click, never at launch.

2

Click Before You're On Camera

The mirror opens under the icon with a fresh compliment underneath. Fix the hair, check the lighting, then click anywhere else or hit Esc. The camera stops immediately.

3

Set It Up Once

Right-click the icon, or use the gear inside the mirror: pick a camera, switch compliments off, launch at login, quit. That's the whole settings menu.

What It Doesn't Do

The short version of the privacy policy.

✗ No recording

Frames go from the camera to the screen and nowhere else. Nothing is written to disk, not even temporarily.

✗ No network

The app ships without the network entitlement. It couldn't phone home if it wanted to.

✗ No account

Nothing to sign up for. Nothing to log in to. No email needed, ever.

✗ No analytics

App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, because that's exactly what happens.

Good to Know

Does it record or save anything?

No. The preview is live video on its way to your screen and nothing more. There's no snapshot button, no save-to-disk path, and no network entitlement in the app at all.

Does the camera stay on in the background?

It can't. The capture session starts when the mirror opens and is torn down when it closes. The green light next to your camera is the honest indicator — watch it go out.

Will it work with my webcam or iPhone?

Yes. Built-in cameras, USB webcams, and iPhone via Continuity Camera all show up in the picker, and the list updates as you plug things in or wake your phone.

Why $1 instead of free?

Because a dollar covers the developer account and keeps the app free of ads, upsells, and a "Pro" tier that unlocks the parts you actually wanted. You pay once and get everything, including whatever gets added later.

Can I turn the compliments off?

You can, in the menu. The mirror works fine on its own. But they're the reason the app exists, so give them a week first.
