LilMirror Support

Check yourself before the call.

Camera not showing up, or something else? Email support@lilstudio.app. We read every message.

Getting started

  • LilMirror has no Dock icon and no window. Look for the camera icon in the menu bar.
  • Click it for the mirror and a compliment. Click anywhere else, or press Esc, and the camera turns off.
  • Right-click it (or use the gear in the mirror) for Camera, Compliments, Launch at Login and Quit.

Common questions

The mirror is black.

LilMirror doesn't have camera access. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera and turn on LilMirror. If another app is using the camera, close it and try again.

How do I use my iPhone or a USB webcam?

Right-click the icon › Camera and pick it. Cameras show up as you plug them in or wake your iPhone (Continuity Camera).

Is the camera on in the background?

No. It starts when the mirror opens and stops the moment it closes. The green light next to your camera tells you the truth.

Can I turn the compliments off?

Right-click › Compliments. The mirror works fine without them.

Does it record or upload anything?

No. There's no snapshot button, nothing is saved, and the app has no network access. See the privacy policy.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click the icon, choose Quit LilMirror, then drag the app from Applications to the Trash.

System requirements

macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel, and any camera.
